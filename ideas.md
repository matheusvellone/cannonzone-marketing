# Ideas

Parked ideas. Product ideas wait for player demand (see `strategy.md`, "Don't fall into the feature trap").

## Growth (cheap, no new mechanics)

- **A shareable link per level** ("beat my level"): already exists (`?code=`). Push it harder, e.g. a more visible Share button on the clear card.
- **Daily level**: one featured level a day gives people a reason to come back and a regular thing to post.
- **A clip or share card on clearing a hard level**: players spread the game themselves. Would reuse the replay and record pipeline.
- **itch.io page**: upload the HTML5 build. Low effort, real discovery.

## Product (only if players ask for it)

- **Moving cannon** ("Canhão se movendo" in the Notion backlog): the iconic DK barrel look. A rotating cannon with `lockFire` and a countdown already covers the feel.
- **Checker quirk**: the Publish sweep ignores countdowns and fires a `lockFire` rotating cannon at every angle in its sweep, so it can report false shortcuts. Design around it (keep that cannon's sweep away from the goal). Minor, not fixed.
